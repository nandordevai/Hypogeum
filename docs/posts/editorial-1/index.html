Editorial

Hello and welcome back to the renewed Hypogeum! It’s been a couple of months since I last updated the site. The reason for this was simple: I began to treat writing new posts as a job – an obligation to you, dear reader – and it took a toll on my enthusiasm. To prevent burnout, I needed a break and a rethink of the process.

I decided to switch from a blog format to a webzine. This way, I won’t feel the need to constantly update the site with every new release. It also means that I will have more time and energy to write about other things besides the latest albums – something I have wanted to do for a long time. In this first issue, you will find several columns: Primordial Sounds, focusing on the most famous and important albums of the genre; Penumbra, dealing with other forms of art; The Drivechain, home of industrial music; and The Hum, focusing on drone releases. I will also continue the Buried Finds column, where I will recommend older releases that I enjoy. Alongside these, this issue features an interview with BlackWeald/Old Crypt. I've also shared some of my photos to help get you into the autumn mood. And if you don’t have time to listen to all the recommendations, I made a mixtape featuring some of my favourite producers.

As always, I hope you find this issue useful. You can still find me on the socials, where I post a bit more regularly. And if you want to be featured on this site, feel free to reach out (preferably by email).
