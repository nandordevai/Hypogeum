From time to time I encounter some art that I’d really like to review or recommend to the readers of Hypogeum, but it doesn’t belong to the musical genres I ususally write about. It might be a different kind of music, or not even music but fine arts or literature. This column will be the place for all these random recommendations and reviews. I hope every reader will find something interesting in these articles.

Hollow Press

I have a feeling that I’m preaching to the choir here, but I just can’t skip this. If there’s only one of you among the readers who haven’t heard about Hollow Press and realizes what they have missed, my mission is complete. They sell comics, games, and – maybe their most recognized products – the so-called lore game books. This category is a rather peculiar one: they could be described as manuals for games that were never released. The most famous of them, Vermis will be reviewed later in this column if you still need convincing. But I suggest that just go to their website and order something, anything. You won’t be disappointed.
