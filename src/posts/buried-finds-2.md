Sometimes I just want to lay down in my bed and listen to something that’s a bit “less than music” in the conventional sense, but more than just noise. One of the albums I play on those occasions is **Annuminas** by **Arctica**. It’s soothing, like standing on a hill on a cool autumn day and looking at the forest. Almost one hour of warm and smooth drones that can reset my mind. There are three versions of it: the original (actually remastered) one, the extended edition which contains three more tracks, for the running total of about 4 hours, and the enchanted edition with a softer and lighter sound. All are available from the Bandcamp page.
