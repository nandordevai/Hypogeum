# New releases

In Depths Forgotten by Insectarium – slow soundscapes, huge spaces and underground meditations define Joel Hinkle’s new album.

{% bandcamp 'https://insectarium.bandcamp.com/album/in-depths-forgotten' %}

Wreathed in Serpents, Crowned in Thorns by The Black Monolith –
after just over half a year, we can once again listen to the master of occult dark ambient from Athens. Expect a ritual atmosphere with heavy bass drones and rhythmic noise.

{% bandcamp 'https://the-black-monolith.bandcamp.com/album/wreathed-in-serpents-crowned-in-thorns' %}

Naiad by Ashtoreth & Penumbral Aethyr – a new ritual album born from the collaboration of two Belgian artists.
