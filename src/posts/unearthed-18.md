In the previous Unearthed columns I reviewed and recommended new releases, but since more than half a year has passed since the last one, I’ll be a bit more lax with dates here.

Let’s start with **Beyond the Nuummite Cosmos** by **Brotherhood of Sleep**. It confuses me a bit, because the Bandcamp page says that it’s their debut album – however, I think I already reviewed their previous one in [Unearthed #14](/posts/unearthed-14/). But let’s let that go and listen to this ritual, ceremonial masterpiece.

<!-- more -->

{% bandcamp 'https://darkodysseyrecords.bandcamp.com/album/beyond-the-nuummite-cosmos' %}

One of my favourite releases of the summer was **The Yasu Ether Project** by **Mount Shrine**. It’s the last release from Cesar Alexandre, who passed away in 2021. It had previously appeared on his Bandcamp page, but that’s now closed. It was re-released on Cryo Chamber, and you should definitely listen to it.

{% bandcamp 'https://cryochamber.bandcamp.com/album/the-yasu-ether-project' %}

For lovers of space/sci-fi ambient blended with industrial atmospheres, I can recommend **Specimen 7** by **Void Stasis**. It would definitely work as background music for the Mothership RPG.

{% bandcamp 'https://cryochamber.bandcamp.com/album/specimen-7' %}

**Tragedy Ecstasy Doom** is a more recent release from **Desiderii Marginis**. Five drone tracks, each over ten minutes. Definitely something to immerse yourself in. Perfect for the coming long autumn evenings.

{% bandcamp 'https://desiderii.bandcamp.com/album/tragedy-ecstasy-doom' %}

I wrote a review of **New Risen Throne**’s previous album, [The Journey to Reach the Fathers](https://www.google.com/search?q=/posts/passing-through-five-portals). The new release, titled **Of Gray Grounds**, won’t get the same attention, unfortunately, as I spent most of my available time getting Hypogeum up and running again. But this album is surely worth mentioning. It’s just as great as the previous one, if not even better. I bought the CD for my collection, and you should too.

{% bandcamp 'https://eighthtowerrecords.bandcamp.com/album/of-gray-grounds' %}

I know that **Magna Silentia** by **Arcana** pushes the limits of dark ambient but this album just can’t be left off this list. Released on October 1, it’s their first album since 2012. Well, it was worth the wait. It’s everything you could expect and more – deep soundscapes, rumbling percussion, touching vocals, and a long journey across the magical world of Arcana. Give it a listen, you won’t regret it.

{% bandcamp 'https://brighterdeathnow.bandcamp.com/album/magna-silentia' %}

**Kloob** released **Iter Ad Psychen** recently. I’ll just quote the Bandcamp page here because it’s beautifully written:

“Across eight pieces, the boundary between waking and dreaming, self and other, future and past gradually dissolves. Invisible signals become transcendent frequencies. Forms emerge without origin. Unknown creatures stir within. The deeper the journey moves, the less certain the idea of a single, fixed identity becomes.”

Everything I like is here: soundscapes, drones, cinematic ambient. Highly recommended.

{% bandcamp 'https://darkodysseyrecords.bandcamp.com/album/iter-ad-psychen' %}

And another release from him: **Echoes Of Dimensional Contemplation**. I won’t repeat myself, go and check this one out, too.

{% bandcamp 'https://winter-light.bandcamp.com/album/echoes-of-dimensional-contemplation' %}

Another deep space ambient album from **Ruptured World**: **Spacefold** is another one you shouldn’t miss if you’re looking for something to listen to during those rainy autumn afternoons.

{% bandcamp 'https://cryochamber.bandcamp.com/album/spacefold' %}
