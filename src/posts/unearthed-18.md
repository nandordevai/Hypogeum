In the previous Unearthed columns I reviewed and recommended new releases, but since more than half year passed since the last one, I’ll be a bit more lax with dates here.

Let’s start with **Beyond the Nuummite Cosmos** by **Brotherhood of Sleep**. It confuses me a bit, because the Bandcamp page says that it’s their debut album – however, I think I already reviewed their previous one in [Unearthed #14](https://hypogeum.hu/posts/unearthed-14/). But let it go and listen to this ritual, ceremonial masterpiece.

<!-- more -->

{% bandcamp 'https://darkodysseyrecords.bandcamp.com/album/beyond-the-nuummite-cosmos' %}

One of my favourite releases of the summer was **The Yasu Ether Project**
by **Mount Shrine**. It’s the last release from Cesar Alexandre, who passed away in 2021. It had previously appeared on his Bandcamp page, but that’s now closed. It was released on Cryo Chamber again and you should definitely listen to it.

{% bandcamp 'https://cryochamber.bandcamp.com/album/the-yasu-ether-project' %}

For lovers of space/sci-fi ambient blended with some industrial atmospheres, I can recommend **Specimen 7** by **Void Stasis**. Definitely would work as a background music for Mothership RPG.

{% bandcamp 'https://cryochamber.bandcamp.com/album/specimen-7' %}

**Tragedy Ecstasy Doom** is a more recent release from **Desiderii Marginis**. Five drone tracks, each one is over ten minutes. Definitely something to immerse yourself into. Perfect for the coming long autumn evenings.

{% bandcamp 'https://desiderii.bandcamp.com/album/tragedy-ecstasy-doom' %}

I wrote a review about **New Risen Throne**’s previous album, [The Journey to Reach the Fathers](/posts/passing-through-five-portals). The new release, titled **Of Gray Grounds** won’t get the same attention unfortunately, as I spent most of my time available to Hypogeum to get it up and running again. But this album surely worth mentioning. It’s just as great as the previous one, if not even better. I bought the CD to have it in my collection and you should, too.

{% bandcamp 'https://eighthtowerrecords.bandcamp.com/album/of-gray-grounds' %}

I know that **Magna Silentia** from **Arcana** is pushing the limits of dark ambient, but this album just can’t be left out from this list. Released on October 1, it’s the first album since 2012. Well, it’s worth the wait. It’s everything you expected and more – deep soundscapes, rumbling percussions, touching vocals, and a long journey across the magical world of Arcana. Go with it, you won’t regret.

{% bandcamp 'https://brighterdeathnow.bandcamp.com/album/magna-silentia' %}

**Kloob** released **Iter Ad Psychen** recently. I’ll just quote the Bandcamp page here because it’s beautifully written:

“Across eight pieces, the boundary between waking and dreaming, self and other, future and past gradually dissolves. Invisible signals become transcendent frequencies. Forms emerge without origin. Unknown creatures stir within. The deeper the journey moves, the less certain the idea of a single, fixed identity becomes.”

Everything is here that I like: soundscapes, drones, cinematic ambient. Highly recommended.

{% bandcamp 'https://darkodysseyrecords.bandcamp.com/album/iter-ad-psychen' %}

Another deep space ambient album from **Ruptured World**: **Spaceworld** is another one that you shouldn’t miss if you’re looking for something to listen to during those rainy autumn afternoons.

{% bandcamp 'https://cryochamber.bandcamp.com/album/spacefold' %}
