Hello and welcome back to the renewed Hypogeum! It’s been a couple of months since I last updated the site. The reason for this was simple: I began to treat writing new posts as a job – an obligation to you, dear reader – and it took a toll on my enthusiasm. To prevent burnout, I needed a break and a rethink of the process.

<!-- more -->

I decided to switch from a blog format to a webzine. This way, I won’t feel the need to constantly update the site with every new release. It also means that I will have more time and energy to write about other things besides the latest albums – something I have wanted to do for a long time. In this first issue, you will find some new columns: Primordial Sounds, focusing on the most famous and important albums of the genre and Penumbra, dealing with other forms of art. I will also continue the Buried Finds column, where I will recommend older releases that I enjoy. And if you don’t have time to listen to all the recommendations, I made a mixtape featuring some of my favourite producers.

Other columns are also planned: I want to introduce The Drivechain in a future issue which will be the home of industrial music, and The Hum, focusing on drone releases. I will also continue interviewing artists.
