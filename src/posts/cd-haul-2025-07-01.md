Lustmord can safely be called a legend, and his 2024 [Much Unseen Is Also Here](https://lustmord.bandcamp.com/album/much-unseen-is-also-here) album is worthy of that title in every moment. He was featured on the [previous mixtape](https://www.mixcloud.com/hypogeum_hu/hypogeum-1/) as well, and will appear on the upcoming second one too.

> “My music is not meant to be explained - only listened to as a means of exposing the sheer insignificance of our primitive thoughts and actions within the vast scale of the cosmos - a scale which we as a species are ill equipped to comprehend.”
