**A Woman Under the Influence** by **Her Menacing Pet** is not only a great dark ambient album with its disturbing drones and haunting sounds, but it’s also available as a name-your-price digital album and includes a copy of **Avocado 1970**, a DIY digital zine focused on dark ambient/experimental music, underground labels, and visual art. And if that’s still not enough, your payment for the album will be donated to Farmaste Animal Sanctuary, a farm animal rescue operation.

{% bandcamp 'https://hermenacingpet.bandcamp.com/album/a-woman-under-the-influence' %}

<!-- more -->

**Haunted Forest** by **Overclouds** is a slow, experimental, dark ambient album with plenty of spoken word (by Shane Beck). They are “guiding you through a disturbed mind that has gone numb,” and that sums it up perfectly.

{% bandcamp 'https://noctivagantcollective.bandcamp.com/album/haunted-forest' %}

**Sphäre Sechs** released its seventh studio album, **Nebular Resonance**. Eight tracks of dark cosmic ambient by Martin and Christian.

{% bandcamp 'https://phelios.bandcamp.com/album/nebular-resonance' %}

**Morwyn - The Vengeance of God** is a collaboration between **Dodenskald** and **Philippe Blache**. An immersive, dark cinematic release based on a novel by John Cowper Powys that was originally titled *Hell*. Even without reading the book, it sounds like a proper tribute. Highly recommended.

{% bandcamp 'https://noctivagantcollective.bandcamp.com/album/morwyn-the-vengeance-of-god' %}

Another collab: **Inade** and **Troum** released **TO PAN EON** on the blessed day of March 1. It’s such a wonderful album that I don’t even want to write anything more. Go and listen. Now.

{% bandcamp 'https://troum.bandcamp.com/album/to-pan-eon-inade-troum' %}

A new album/track from **Torus Dome**: **Shadow Haunted Chambers** is an extended version of the “Shadow Haunted Chambers” track taken from the **Invincible Order** album.

{% bandcamp 'https://torusdome.bandcamp.com/album/shadow-haunted-chambers' %}

**Deceptions of False Light** by **Dead Angel Ensemble** (Shane Beck and Olik Nesnah) is “the tale of the obliterated soul, a wasted life destroyed by dead empathy,” as they say in the release notes. It’s a long, melancholic journey through bleak soundscapes, accompanied by spoken word, slow drones, and occasional strange noises.

{% bandcamp 'https://noctivagantcollective.bandcamp.com/album/deceptions-of-false-light' %}

Let’s move into the realm of noise. **An Exceptional Occurrence Of Dread** by **Mortum** is an album that merges the heaviness of death industrial with the atmosphere of dark ambient. George Thuroczy uses custom-built hardware to create a “raw, unmediated transmission of analog darkness” – and he does it with great mastery.

{% bandcamp 'https://arcanedirge.bandcamp.com/album/an-exceptional-occurrence-of-dread' %}

**Star Shaped Chimney** released **Deadly Cellar**, a two-track horror/dark ambient album featuring analogue synths and cinematic compositions.

{% bandcamp 'https://starshapedchimney.bandcamp.com/album/deadly-cellar' %}

Let’s finish this Unearthed with a ritual, neo-shamanistic album: **Avàvâgo Zamran!** by **ARROKOTH**. It features four long, ceremonial tracks (or perhaps it's better to call them acts), played mostly on flutes, gongs, and hand drums, with added vocals. These pieces can accompany any mind-altering activities – or can even be such activities themselves.

{% bandcamp 'https://cycliclaw.bandcamp.com/album/ava-va-go-zamran' %}
