Welcome to the first installment of the new column, **Buried Finds**. Here we focus backward in time, digging deeper into the silt. I’ll recommend a couple of older releases in each post and some extra in dedicated [Patreon posts](https://www.patreon.com/c/Hypogeum) available for subscribers.

Our first one is **Oblio** by **Harrogat**, released in December 2, 2024. An album running over one hour, featuring only one track but lots of subtlety.

{% bandcamp 'https://lakelabel.bandcamp.com/album/oblio' %}

**Æscesi**’s **Clausurah** is a mystical, ritual dark ambient release. It features eight numbered pieces, filled with rich textures and deep growling noises. Listening to it feels like visiting an ancient monastery that’s devoted to a god you’ve never heard about before – and one who clearly doesn’t care about your happiness…

{% bandcamp 'https://dapalissepolcri.bandcamp.com/album/clausurah' %}
