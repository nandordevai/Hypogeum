Welcome to the first installment of the new column, **Buried Finds**. Here we focus backward in time, digging deeper into the silt. I’ll recommend a couple of older releases in each post and some extra in dedicated [Patreon posts](https://www.patreon.com/c/Hypogeum) available for subscribers.

Our first one is **Oblio** by **Harrogat**, released in December 2, 2024. An album running over one hour, featuring only one track but lots of subtlety.
