# Hollow Press

I have a feeling that I’m preaching to the choir here, but I just can’t skip this. If there’s only one of you among the readers who haven’t heard about  [Hollow Press](https://hollow-press.net) and realizes what they have missed, my mission is complete. They sell comics, games, and – maybe their most recognized products – the so-called lore game books. This category is a rather peculiar one: they could be described as manuals for games that were never released. The most famous of them, [Vermis](https://hollow-press.net/collections/lore-game-books/products/vermis-i) will be reviewed later in this column if you still need convincing. But I suggest that just go to their website and order something, anything. You won’t be disappointed.
